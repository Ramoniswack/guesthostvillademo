About G’Host Villa · Sarangkot, Pokhara

G’Host = your gracious host.

Perched along the peaceful Sarangkot / Ranipauwa ridge just 20 minutes above Lakeside Pokhara, The G’Host Villa Restro and Bar blends hillside calm, signature architecture and warm Nepalese hospitality.

Across our Crystal Pyramid Dome House and Main Villa, you can check into a quiet room for two or gather up to 24 guests for a private celebration, retreat or unhurried family weekend.

How we host

Simple comforts, done properly.

Space to breathe

Set along the quiet Sarangkot ridge above Pokhara, surrounded by pine trees, fresh air and wide valley views.

Pyramid restro & bottle bar

Daily breakfast, Nepali and Newari favourites, wood-fired plates, BBQ platters and drinks under the pyramid roof.

Built for couples & groups

Book a single mountain-view room for two, a private villa floor for six, or take over all 7BHK for up to 24.

Direct, personal hosting

No middlemen or automated desks—message our Sarangkot team directly on WhatsApp and we shape the stay around you.

The rhythm of a stay

A day at G’Host Villa.

View food & drinks menu
01 · Morning

Sunrise & slow breakfast

Wake up to golden light over the Annapurna range and Phewa Valley, then walk over to the pyramid for hot breakfast and coffee.

02 · Afternoon

Hilltop calm or Lakeside

Spend quiet hours in the garden and sun deck, or ride 20 minutes down the scenic road to Lakeside whenever you feel like exploring.

03 · Evening

Pyramid bar & bonfire

Watch Pokhara’s valley lights come alive over dinner, handcrafted drinks, and an outdoor BBQ or evening bonfire in the garden.
