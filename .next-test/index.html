Need to know

Quick answers.

What room types and BHK configurations are available?+

We offer both individual 1 BHK Couple Deluxe suites (~260–280 sq.ft) with private balconies, as well as multi-bedroom configurations including 4-BHK villa wings (~1,450 sq.ft) and a 7-BHK full private villa buyout (~2,800 sq.ft, up to 9 rooms) accommodating 20 to 24 guests.

What are the room rates and group packages?+

Couple Deluxe Rooms are priced around NPR 6,800 to NPR 7,200 per night (including breakfast). For groups of 4 or more, we offer shared packages starting at NPR 1,550 per person including welcome drinks and breakfast.

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Check-in is from 2:00 PM to 11:00 PM and check-out is from 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM. If you need early arrival or late departure, let us know via WhatsApp and we will accommodate where possible.

What in-room amenities are provided?+

Every room includes air conditioning, attached bathroom with 24/7 hot water, free Wi-Fi, private scenic balcony with mountain or garden views, electric kettle with tea/coffee maker, slippers, fresh toiletries, and ample wall charging sockets.
